About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in Bioengineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), under the supervision of Prof. Yang Liu. Prior to this, I completed my Master's degree at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) under the supervision of Prof. Yiming Li, where I worked on developing efficient computational methods for single-molecule localization microscopy. During my undergraduate studies at Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine, I conducted research on antenatal cardiotocography signal classification under the supervision of Prof. Hang Wei.

My current research interests lie in computational microscopy, super-resolution imaging, and machine learning for quantitative biological imaging.
