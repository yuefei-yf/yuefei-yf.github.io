About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Bioengineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research interests lie at the intersection of computational microscopy, advanced optical imaging, and machine learning.

I am particularly interested in developing computational methods for quantitative biological imaging, with a focus on single-molecule localization microscopy and high-resolution fluorescence microscopy.
